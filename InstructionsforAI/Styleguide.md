# Starcup — Visual Identity & Design System

## 1. Brand Overview
**Starcup** is an innovative university startup-building platform and event series. The visual identity bridges a welcoming, casual startup atmosphere (coffee & networking) with cutting-edge tech, data analytics, and gamified investments.

---

## 2. Color Palette
The palette relies on clean, high-contrast backgrounds paired with deep indigo text and vibrant purple-to-violet gradient accents.

*   **Primary Dark (Text / Headers):** `#1E1B4B` (Deep Indigo / Dark Navy)
*   **Background Base (Light):** `#F8FAFC` (Clean Soft White / Slate 50)
*   **Card / Surface Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White with subtle shadow & glass border)
*   **Brand Accent (Primary Purple):** `#6366F1` to `#7C3AED` (Vibrant Violet / Indigo Gradient)
*   **Success / Metric Accent:** `#10B981` (Emerald Green for positive stats and growth)

---

## 3. Typography
Modern, geometric, clean sans-serif typefaces ensuring high legibility across web platforms and presentation slides.

*   **Primary Font Family:** Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, orsystem-ui sans-serif.
*   **Headings (H1, H2):** Bold / Extra Bold (`font-weight: 700` or `800`), colored in Primary Dark (`#1E1B4B`).
*   **Subtitles & Highlights:** Medium weight (`font-weight: 500`), styled in Brand Accent Purple (`#7C3AED`).
*   **Body Text:** Regular (`font-weight: 400`), colored in Slate Gray (`#475569`).

---

## 4. Logo & Glassmorphism Style
*   **Logo Composition:** Icon on the left, brand name typography ("Starcup") on the right.
*   **Icon Style:** Minimalist 3D-effect frosted glass icon featuring a purple star fused with a coffee cup shape and soft vapor steam rising above.
*   **Glassmorphism UI Rules:**
    *   `background`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
    *   `backdrop-filter`: `blur(12px)`
    *   `border`: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`
    *   `box-shadow`: `0 10px 30px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`

---

## 5. UI Components & Elements

### Buttons
*   **Primary Button:** Solid vibrant purple gradient (`#6366F1` to `#7C3AED`), rounded corners (`border-radius: 12px`), white text, and a smooth right-arrow icon (`→`).
*   **Hover State:** Slight scale-up (`scale(1.02)`) with enhanced drop shadow.

### Cards & Dashboards
*   Clean floating cards over a soft grid or gradient mesh background.
*   Widgets featuring key metrics (e.g., Lead counts, Conversion percentages, Growth indicators) highlighted with green success badges (`+34%`).
